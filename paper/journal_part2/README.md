## Scope

Part II takes the risk surface derived in Part I into the response layer:

- the flood-aware road graph and edge-cost formulation
- routing outcome: exposure removed against detour incurred
- the survivorship-corrected 7x7 sweep of the two cost constants
- the search-transparent Dijkstra vs A* benchmark, including the wall-clock
  result that contradicts the node-count result
- the QUBO/QAOA dispatch layer, verified against the Hungarian optimum

It reads as a continuation: the study area, satellite inputs and the
construction of `S(x)` and `T(t)` are not repeated, only cited.
