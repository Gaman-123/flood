## Scope

Part I builds and stress-tests the static half of the two-stage risk model
`R(x,t) = S(x)·T(t)`:

- SAR flood-frequency inventory under the twelve-day single-orbit constraint
- conditioning factors, collinearity screening, terrain-matched negative sampling
- the NDVI/land-cover confound and its removal by ablation
- classifier, isotonic calibration and the prior-correction analysis
- the three-stage validation ladder: random split, spatial-block CV, geographic transfer
- the dynamic trigger `T(t)` and the sweep of its own constants

Part II applies the resulting surface to routing and dispatch.

## Do not edit this file directly

Both parts are generated from `../paper_journal_v2.tex` by
`scripts/split_journal.py`. Edit the source manuscript and re-run:

```bash
python scripts/split_journal.py
```

The splitter lifts section bodies verbatim and authors only the connective
tissue (preamble, abstract, introduction, conclusions). It also handles the
things that break when a one-column manuscript becomes two-column: promoting
wide tables and figures to their starred full-width form, reflowing display
equations that were set for a full-page measure, converting `\textwidth` to
`\linewidth` inside floats, and redirecting references whose target moved into
the companion paper. It fails loudly on an unresolved reference rather than
emitting a `??`.
